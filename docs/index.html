Markets

Most likely is not the same as best value. Likelier outcomes pay lower odds, and in backtests even the safest picks lost money on average after the bookmaker's margin. A pick only has value if your bookmaker offers more than its fair odds.

    Remember

    Probabilities are calibrated estimates, not guarantees. In historical backtests the bookmakers' own prices were at least as accurate as this model, and betting on the model's "edge" lost money on average. Use the fair odds as a reference, treat any gap to your bookmaker's price with caution, and never stake more than you can afford to lose.
